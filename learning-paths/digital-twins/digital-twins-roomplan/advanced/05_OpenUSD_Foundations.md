# Week 5 – OpenUSD Foundations (Theory)

**Time:** 4 hours  
**Type:** Theory  
**Goal:** Learn the core concepts of OpenUSD so you can compose and enrich RoomPlan outputs.

| Block              | Duration | Activity |
|--------------------|----------|----------|
| NVIDIA Learning Path | 2 h 30 m | Foundations modules |
