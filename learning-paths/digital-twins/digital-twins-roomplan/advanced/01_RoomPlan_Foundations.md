## Learning Objectives
By the end of this week you should be able to answer:

- What is the difference between parametric data and mesh output?
- Which architectural elements and object categories does RoomPlan detect?
- What are the recommended room size and lighting conditions?
- What does `CapturedRoom` contain (walls, doors, windows, openings, objects, transforms)?
- What are the main limitations for residential vs larger spaces?
